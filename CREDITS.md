## Map art

The island (since the third map) is built from **The Fan-tasy Tileset**:
the Premium pack and its Snow Adventures, Desert Oasis, Turning of the
Seasons and Castles and Fortresses expansions. Only the packed tiles and
pictures the map uses are in `assets/maps/` (generated by
`scripts/fantasy/build3.py`, which reads the packs from a local copy;
the packs themselves are not in this repository). The palace, towers and
fountain are cut from the pack's own sample maps.
The Shadow Crystal is its large violet Magic Crystal prop (`assets/ui/shadow_crystal.png`).

The earlier maps (`assets/maps/almia.json`) use Sunnyside World by Daniel Diggle.
